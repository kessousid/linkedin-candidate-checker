Bulk LinkedIn Backfill

Pulls real candidates from Curatal who don't have a LinkedIn URL yet, searches LinkedIn for each by name, then works through the name-matching results in batches of 3: first checks just Experience (full history, not just the current headline) against the company on file in Curatal — if exactly one profile in the batch agrees, that's the match. If that's ambiguous or empty, it re-checks the same 3 profiles adding Education into the mix (a profile now counts if either signal agrees), which can pull apart an ambiguous pair or catch a stale company. Still unresolved? Moves to the next 3 name-matches and repeats — up to 2 batches (6 profiles) per candidate. Anything still uncertain after that is left alone rather than guessed. Runs one candidate at a time with pauses in between (not a burst of requests), and keeps running in the background even if you close this tab. Note: visiting full profiles (not just search results) counts against LinkedIn's monthly profile-view limit for non-premium accounts more than searching alone does.

"Recheck no-matches" re-runs just the candidates from the batch below that came back no_match/error, in place -- no re-fetch from Curatal, no change to the resume cursor. Use it after a matching-logic fix, or just to give a borderline case a second pass.
